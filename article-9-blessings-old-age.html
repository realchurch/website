9 Blessings Needed in Old Age

By Rev. Choi Jong-in | November 25, 2024

I have long been interested in ministry for the elderly. Peace Church's ministry for the elderly proceeds in three directions: ministry within the church, community outreach, and nursing home missions.

In old age, we experience inevitable transitions. It is best to spend old age in one's own home ('Aging in Place'), but we must prepare for the blessings needed in this season of life.

1. Blessing of Health

Spending a healthy old age is everyone's dream. However, 9 out of 10 elderly people face health challenges. Life expectancy is 82.4 years, but healthy life expectancy is only 64.9 years. We must receive the blessing of health, praying like John: "Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers" (3 John 1:2).

2. Blessing of Work

The elderly need work not just for income, but to confirm their social roles and values. Moses was a shepherd at 80, Daniel served as prime minister past 80, and Caleb at 85 asked for pioneer land. Through work, the elderly maintain dignity and purpose.

3. Blessing of Family

Family provides psychological stability and comfort for the elderly. Like Cornelius's family that feared God and prayed together, healthy families become safe havens where the elderly find peace and belonging.

4. Blessing of Finances

Economic independence in old age protects dignity and enables service. Like Barzillai who helped King David, elderly saints need financial blessings to live abundantly and serve others.

5. Blessing of Faith

Having faith in old age is essential for 'successful aging.' Faith strengthens well-being, reduces depression, and helps interpret life's challenges through a religious lens. The fellowship of faith is vital for a successful old age.

6. Blessing of Friends

Good friends increase happiness, reduce stress, and help cope with life transitions. Like Daniel's three friends, we need companions who share our faith, pray together, and endure trials alongside us.

7. Blessing of Dwelling

A stable dwelling is crucial for the elderly. Like Abraham's tent where hospitality was practiced and couples aged together, our dwelling should be a place of welcome, health, and companionship.

8. Blessing of Wisdom

Discernment is essential in old age. We need wisdom to make clear judgments, consume information wisely, and maintain dignity. The blessing of wisdom helps us navigate the complexities of aging.

9. Blessing of Heaven

Finishing life well is crucial. Just as we prepare to move homes, we must prepare to move to heaven. The hope of heaven gives meaning to our final season. I recommend going to heaven together—this is the ultimate blessing we can share.

Materials are abundant, but the best thing a pastor can give is blessing. Therefore, proclaim these 9 blessings of old age and pray powerfully for them to come.

Rev. Choi Jong-in [Peace Church / Editorial Writer]
