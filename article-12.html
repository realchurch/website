7 Faiths to Have During Financial Crisis

Rev. Choi Jong-in | January 6, 2023

We need faith to overcome financial crises. God is unchanging and can sustain us in any situation.

1. Bring Your Fear and Anxiety to God

God cares deeply about our suffering. Cast your anxiety on God. "Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you" (1 Peter 5:7).

2. Live with Stewardship Consciousness

We are stewards of the money and resources God has given us. The ability to make money and build wealth comes from God. Put your hope in God, not in wealth.

3. Hold Onto God Tighter

In times of crisis, you must hold onto God even tighter. Will God, who feeds the birds of the air, not care for His children? God is greater than supply chains and recessions.

4. Practice Contentment in Good and Bad Times

Paul learned the secret of being content in any and every situation, whether well fed or hungry (Phil 4:12-13). Trusting in God's provision leads to contentment with what we have.

5. Be Generous Even in Crisis

Open your wallet even more when times are hard. Generosity is a powerful antidote to fear. When we give, God delights in our generosity and fills us.

6. Pray in Times of Crisis

Paul met an angel of God through prayer even in the storm. When we pray in limit situations where our knowledge and experience are useless, God's work happens.

7. Dig Many Ditches in the Valley

In times of crisis, there is nothing I can do. We must expect God's help and dig ditches of faith. God provides miraculously beyond our thoughts and methods (2 Kings 3:16-20).
