End-of-Life Coaching

Well-Dying Coaching

A handbook for pastoral counselors, chaplains, and senior care professionals in end-of-life coaching

웰다잉 코칭

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

A handbook of end-of-life and well-dying coaching for pastoral counselors, chaplains, and senior care professionals. Anchored in Christian theology and joined with professional coaching methodology, it works systematically from clarifying values and advance care planning, through life review and legacy work, coaching through the transition to terminal illness and for family systems and caregivers, to bereavement and the training of trainers.
