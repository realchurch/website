Scripture and Care

End-of-Life Told by Luke

The last season of life read through nine scenes from Luke's Gospel

누가가 들려주는 End-of-Life

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

Through nine scenes from the Gospel of Luke, written by a physician, this book illumines the last season of life biblically and as a whole person. It follows Simeon's peaceful departure, the prayer in Gethsemane, grace spoken on the cross, and the hope of the resurrection on the road to Emmaus, and it treats end-of-life care, bereavement counseling, and the ethical stance of those who minister.
