Hospice Guide

A practical guide for patients, families, and those who keep watch beside them

호스피스 가이드

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

A practical guide for patients facing a life-threatening illness, their families, and the pastors and church care volunteers who keep watch beside them. It introduces hospice not as a surrender of treatment but as care that protects the dignity and peace of the days that remain, and it guides readers broadly through home, inpatient, and consultative hospice; home safety; pain and symptom management; spiritual care; and the final hours and bereavement.
