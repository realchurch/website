Theology of Aging

Christian Spirituality in Later Life

The spiritual journey of old age, read again in a super-aged society

노년기 기독교 신앙과 영성

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

In a super-aged society, this book explores old age not as mere decline but as a spiritual journey in which the whole of a life is read again and matures before God. Moving between gerontological theory, biblical theology, and meditation on classical paintings, it sets out a ministry of later life for the Korean church that reaches from rebuilding identity after retirement, through spirituality amid dementia, illness, and disability, to preparing for death and moving from caregiving to partnership.
