Bible Study for Later Life

Confidence in Old Age Learned from Daniel

Twelve Bible-study lessons on purpose and dignity in later life

다니엘에게 배우는 노년의 자신감

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

A twelve-lesson Bible study drawn from the life of Daniel, who kept an unshaken faith from his youth into old age, to help older believers live on with purpose and dignity before God. Gerontological theory (ego integrity, continuity, and gerotranscendence) is set alongside four movements in each lesson: opening the heart, entering the Word, feeling it in life, and decision and prayer.
