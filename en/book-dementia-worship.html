Dementia Care

Dementia-friendly Worship

The dignity of worship remains even when memory fades

치매 친화적 예배

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

This practical book on worship begins from the conviction that even when memory fades, the relationship with God and the dignity of worship do not disappear. It gathers principles by which members with dementia and their families share grace together — short spans of attention, familiar hymns and the Lord's Prayer, a comfortable worship space, sermons and communion that engage the senses — together with a twelve-week worship plan for Korean churches.
