Cancer-Friendly Church

A church's healing strategy for embracing cancer patients and their families

암환자 친화적 교회

By Rev. Choi Jong-in

Overview

A guide to healing companionship that asks how a church should keep watch beside a member and family who have received a cancer diagnosis. It sets out a church practice that holds the whole journey: refraining from hasty interpretations and wounding words and staying with listening; practical support for daily life; care for caregivers and families; and the survivor's life through diagnosis, treatment, recurrence, and recovery.
