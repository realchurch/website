7 Reasons Why Giving Offering is Good

Rev. Choi Jong-in | January 13, 2023

Why should we give money to support the church? Philippians 4 shows us that offering and a generous spirit are beneficial to us.

1. Giving is Merciful

Offering is a merciful act that relieves the suffering of ministers and neighbors. The giving love of saints is a great strength to missionaries and ministry fields.

2. Offering is Ministry

Giving is not just a way to support ministry, but participating in the ministry itself. The Philippians participated in Paul's ministry through their offering.

3. Giving is Wise

Investing in the gospel ministry is the safest and wisest investment, storing up treasures in heaven. It is an investment that yields eternal returns.

4. Giving is Worship

Offering is a fragrant offering that pleases God. We worship when we give. Offering is an expression of sincere worship to the Lord.

5. Giving is Safe

"And my God will meet all your needs" (Phil 4:19). Investing in the kingdom of God is very safe, and God will meet our needs.

6. I Give but God Takes Responsibility

When we give, God takes responsibility. God is a loving Father who knows our needs exactly and leads us in good ways.

7. Giving Offering Glorifies God

Our greatest hope is to glorify God. If we can give by God's grace, it is a way to honor Him. Everything is for the glory of God.
