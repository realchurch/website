Crown of Righteousness, 2 Timothy 4:6-8

Rev. Choi Jong-in | October 21, 2022

Knowing his death was imminent, Apostle Paul left words like a testament to his son in faith, Timothy.This is the final image that all saints should emulate.

1. Fight the Good Fight

Life is a series of battles. However, saints must fight the good fight of faith, not against flesh and blood. A life fighting against sin, temptation, and for God's glory is blessed.

2. Finish the Race

Religious life is like a marathon. It is important not to give up halfway but to finish the race. Run silently on the path of mission God has set for you.

3. Keep the Faith

Keeping unchanging faith amidst the storms of the world is the greatest victory. Trust God and hold onto His promises in any situation.

4. Look Forward to the Crown of Righteousness

A crown of righteousness is reserved for those who triumph by faith. Live hoping for eternal heavenly rewards, not earthly ones. The Lord will praise you, "Well done, good and faithful servant."
